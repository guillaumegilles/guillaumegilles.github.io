## Module 8 / Final exam (EF 2025-2026)
- EX1: firm needs 1 000 000 €. Bank A simple 6 %/yr; Bank B compound 5 %/yr.
  - Simple → arithmetic; compound → geometric.
  - A: u_n = 1 000 000 + 60 000 n ; over 10 yr → 1 600 000.
  - B: v_n = 1 000 000 × 1.05^n ; over 10 yr → 1 628 895.
  - A cheaper over 10 years.
  - Simple monthly 0.5 % → annual 6 % (proportional, same as A).
  - Compound monthly 0.5 % → annual (1.005^12 − 1) = 6.17 %.
