So Keep it Free?

Because it the BEST way to learn!

Our brain is design to learn with repetition imagine you spend 4 hours on the app:

  • you went through dozen of bite-sized lessons,
  • jump somes, because you are bored.
  • crush multiple exercices one after another,
  • closed the app,
  • come back at it 2 weeks later.

Great, you check everything on todo list for the next 2 weeks. But when you open the app again 2 week later, you feel nervous, i am not sure to remember what i have seen last time. it is daunting. You have enjoyed Premium experience but did you maximize your learning experience?

Free tier force you to go through lessons slowly, one at a time everyday. It helps your brain building connection between concept

What are you trying to accomplish, publish Einstein grade thesis tomorrow. if it were. you wouldn’t need Brilliant in the first place so start learning and using Brilliant how it should be design for learning, 5 minutes everyday with a cup of coffee, or tea. take your time, enjoy a free learning experince.

The goal is not the destination but the journey! Enjoy the ride, savor every daily lessons
