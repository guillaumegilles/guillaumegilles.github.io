Profile

I am an economist, statistician and Python developer working at the intersection of economics, finance, mathematics and machine learning. My experience spans regional economic analysis, financial-risk assessment, statistical modelling, time-series analysis and higher-education teaching.

I am particularly interested in using econometrics, machine learning and deep learning to study complex economic and financial systems. My approach combines economic reasoning, rigorous quantitative methods and clear communication, with an emphasis on models that are robust, interpretable and useful for decision-making.

Research and technical interests

My current interests include:

  • financial econometrics and macro-financial forecasting;

I am especially motivated by interdisciplinary projects that connect methodological depth with concrete economic or financial questions.
