3-Day Timetable (18 h)

Modified

October 26, 2026

Abstract

Full timetable of the course: 3 days of 6 hours of teaching (360 minutes each), 1,080 minutes in total.

How to read this page

  • Each day has 360 minutes of teaching, 1,080 minutes for the whole course. Breaks are listed under each table and are not counted.
  • Each quest opens with a live theory segment of 20 to 30 minutes, included in the block duration. The reference pages are pre-reading (the evening before, 45 minutes at most).
  • In every block the order is: theory, then worked example, then exercise (solution folded).
  • The 1 h and 3 h 30 exams are outside the 18 h (separate session). Only the “boss” cases and XP assess students during the three days: see Assessment.
  • The “Cut first” column says what to drop first if a block overruns, so the day still ends at 6 h.

Day 1 — Exploring the financial statements

Goal: read and check financial statements, understand consolidation, then apply the six steps of the Banque de France method to an SME.

Block Start (min) Duration (min) Goal Sources Cut first
D1-B1 0 15 Kick-off: game rules, teams, overview of the 6 steps Home, BdF method Badge details
D1-B2 15 110 Q1 — Read and check balance sheet, income statement and cash flow (theory 25) Q1, Financial statements Level 3 (extension)
D1-B3 125 100 Q2 — Consolidate a group: scope, eliminations, minority interests (theory 25) Q2, BdF method Intragroup dividends (extension)
D1-B4 225 135 Q3 — Full 6-step diagnosis + Day 1 boss (theory 25) Q3, Performance, Structure, Liquidity DSCR exercise (extension)

Breaks (not counted): 15 min mid-morning, 60 min lunch, 15 min in the afternoon.

Evening preparation (≤ 45 min): choose a real annual report (CAC 40) for Day 2 and read the Performance page.

Day 2 — Performance and strategy

Goal: measure performance, place the company in its business model, then decide on a rating and compare listed and unlisted companies.

Block Start (min) Duration (min) Goal Sources Cut first
D2-B1 0 120 Q4 — ROCE, ROE, DuPont, financial leverage (theory 25) Q4, Performance, Formulas BFR and DuPont (homework)
D2-B2 120 105 Q5 — Business Model Canvas, SWOT, CSR/ESG (theory 20) Q5, BdF method Summary note and slides (homework)
D2-B3 225 135 Q6 — Rating, listed vs unlisted, valuation + Day 2 boss (theory 25) Q6, BdF rating Reporting table (homework)

Breaks (not counted): 15 min mid-morning, 60 min lunch, 15 min in the afternoon.

Evening preparation (≤ 45 min): read the non-financial performance statement (DPEF) or the CSR section of the chosen annual report and note one ESG initiative.

Day 3 — Monitoring, financial strategy and ESG

Goal: integrate regulatory monitoring, build a 3-year financial plan and conclude with a report combining finance and ESG.
