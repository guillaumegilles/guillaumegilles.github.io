Description

After each session, you must take online practice quizzes on Moodle platform. 

Thus, below each session, you will find 3 quizzes, divided into three categories : 

These quizzes are progressive and will open in that particular order upon completion : confirmed level after you have completed beginner level and expert level after you have completed confirmed level. All three must be taken. They allow you to know your level. Your scores will count for up to 10% of your final grade.  We strongly advise you not to take the quizzes in advance as it might be penalizing. 

For each question, clicking on the "Check" button will allow you to see if you chose the right answer. If your answer is wrong, you may review the question and choose a different answer. However, keep in mind that you will receive a penalty if your second choice is wrong again (-0,33% for multiple choice questions, -1pt for True/False question).

Please note that the quizzes will stay open and available for you to practice as many times as you wish. Remember however that it is your first score (attempt) that will be recorded and counted as a grade and that your next attempts will not be taken into account. 

 Reminder ! Once you have completed the quiz click on "Finish attempt" and then on "Submit all and finish".
